# 可数名词及其单复数变化规则与不规则变化

**章节**：第1章 名词 (Noun)
**出处**：手绘语法教材 P1-P3 · 对应外研七上 Starter & Unit 1

## 核心规则
1. 规则变化四步法：一般加 -s；特殊辅音结尾加 -es；辅音+y 变 ies；f/fe 结尾常变 ves (half -> halves, knife -> knives, leaf -> leaves)。
2. 不规则变化核心群：变元音 (man->men, foot->feet, tooth->teeth)；加 -en (child->children)；单复同形 (sheep, deer, Chinese, fish)。
3. 复合名词复数规则：一般仅后词加 -s (apple trees)；由 man/woman 构成的复合词，前后均变复数 (men teachers, women doctors)。

## 易错陷阱
【高频陷阱】：photo->photos, piano->pianos（外来词均加 -s）；German 变复数为 Germans（非 Germen）；stomach 结尾发 /k/ 音，复数直接加 -s (stomachs)。

## 教材引用与例句
### 外研版七上 Starter 课本真实对白
> Mum: Where is your schoolbag? Li Ming: There are two books, three pencils and an eraser in it.
💡 *books 与 pencils 为规则复数加 -s；eraser 属元音音素开头单词用 an。*

### 初中英语语法图解 (P2) 手写精要
> 以 s, x, ch, sh 结尾加 -es (buses, boxes, watches, dishes)；以“辅音+y”结尾变 y 为 i 加 -es (family -> families, story -> stories)。
💡 *元音字母+y结尾直接加 -s (holiday -> holidays, boy -> boys)。*

## 实战演练
### 练习 1
**题目**：There are forty ______ in our school, and three of them are from Germany. They are all friendly ______.
**选项**：
- A. woman teachers; Germans
- B. women teachers; Germans
- C. women teachers; Germen
- D. woman teachers; Germen

**正确答案**：B. women teachers; Germans
**解析**：woman 构成的职业复合名词，前后两词均需变复数：women teachers；German（德国人）变复数直接加 -s (Germans)，不可与 English/French 混淆。故选 B。

### 练习 2
**题目**：The farmer raised fifty ______ and twenty ______ on his green hill.
**选项**：
- A. sheeps; cows
- B. sheep; cows
- C. sheep; cow
- D. deers; cows

**正确答案**：B. sheep; cows
**解析**：sheep（绵羊）与 deer（鹿）均为单复同形名词，复数形式不加 -s；cow（母牛）为规则可数名词，复数加 -s。故选 B。

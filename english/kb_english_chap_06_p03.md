# 非谓语动词：动词不定式 to do 与动名词 doing

**章节**：第6章 动词：情态与非谓语
**出处**：手绘语法教材 P66-P71 · 对应中考语法填空必考

## 核心规则
1. 只接 doing 的常见动词：enjoy, finish, practice, mind, consider, keep, suggest, feel like, look forward to (to 为介词)。
2. 只接 to do 的常见动词：want, hope, wish, decide, refuse, agree, promise, expect, choose, plan。
3. 感官使役动词省 to：see, hear, notice, watch, make, let, have 后面接省略 to 的不定式 (make sb do sth)；但变为被动语态时必须把 to 还原！(He was made to work hard)。

## 易错陷阱
【易混陷阱】：look forward to hearing from you (to 是介词，必须接 hearing，不能接 hear)；forget to close the door (门还没关) vs forget closing the door (关过门但忘了)。

## 教材引用与例句
### 外研七上课文原句
> Emma decided to start a writing club because she enjoys sharing fantasy stories with others.
💡 *decide to do sth (决定做某事接不定式)；enjoy doing sth (喜欢做某事接动名词)。*

### 初中英语速记口诀 (P68)
> enjoy, finish, practice 后接 doing；decide, want, hope, agree 后接 to do；stop to do (停下去做另一件事) vs stop doing (停止正在做的事)。
💡 *remember/forget to do (未做) vs doing (已做)；介词后面一律接 doing！*

## 实战演练
### 练习 1
**题目**：I am really looking forward to ______ my pen pal from Australia this summer holiday.
**选项**：
- A. visit
- B. visiting
- C. visited
- D. to visit

**正确答案**：B. visiting
**解析**：look forward to 中 to 是介词，介词后面必须接动名词 doing (visiting)。故选 B。

### 练习 2
**题目**：The boy was tired, so he stopped ______ a rest under the big tree.
**选项**：
- A. having
- B. to have
- C. have
- D. had

**正确答案**：B. to have
**解析**：stop to do sth 表示停下手中的事情去开始做另一件事（去休息）；stop doing 表示停止当前正在做的事。由语境可知男孩停下来去休息，故选 B。

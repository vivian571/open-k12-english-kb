## 3. 核心短语与高分地道句型速查
- **迫不及待做某事**：`can't wait to do sth.` (*I just couldn't wait to try Sichuan hot pot!*)
- **期待做某事（to 为介词）**：`look forward to doing sth.` (*I'm really looking forward to visiting the museum.*)
- **从年少时起梦想做某事**：`dream of doing sth. from an early age` (*Xu dreamed of visiting China's seas and mountains.*)
- **启程踏上一生的旅程**：`set out on one's life's journey` (*At 22, he set out on his journey.*)
- **入乡随俗**：`When in China, do as the Chinese do!` (借用经典谚语 *When in Rome, do as the Romans do*)
- **双关修辞 (Pun)**：*After lunch, things went from really hot to totally cool.*（hot 兼指火锅麻辣与气氛热烈；cool 兼指展馆凉爽与文物震撼很酷）。
